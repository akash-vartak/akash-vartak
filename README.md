#### PhD Student in Computer Science @ University of Maryland, Baltimore County

Currently researching **how to create contextually coherent and natural looking adversarial patches in images to throw off computer vision models**.

Past work includes **use of Graph Neural Networks as meta-classifiers to detect trojan DNN models** and **LLM-guided label-free open-vocabulary object detection**.

During my Masters I worked on **Using Text to Improve Classification of Man-Made Objects**: [Masters Thesis](https://www.proquest.com/docview/2695026845/A5E4B7523A264CE4PQ/1?accountid=14577).

**I’m always looking to collaborate on Adversarial and Trustworthy ML, and LLM/Agent-guided frameworks.**
